# The web app

zapx live pages (runtime-7) served by zaplinkd, the same on the phone and the PC.

- [x] Agents: each one's state, card, model, tokens and time; start, kill, resume.
- [x] An agent's steps live, as the terminal shows them but laid out to read on a phone; a box to
  send it an order, and quick ones (stop, finish and land, say what you are doing).
- [x] Goals and loops, the queue, the limit and the plan's usage.
- [x] The board, the areas, the loops and goals and the controls as screens.
- [ ] Installable on the phone (PWA) with a notice when an agent ends, stops or asks.
